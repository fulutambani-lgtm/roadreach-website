Best way to see what works for your budget is a 15-min call to match your target areas to the right package."

#### If lead asks about tracking/ROI:
> "Every car is GPS-tracked. We provide route reports showing where your brand travelled, estimated daily impressions by suburb, and campaign analytics. Combine that with your own foot traffic or sales data and you have a measurable campaign. Happy to show you the dashboard on a quick call."

#### If lead worries about brand safety / placement:
> "All wraps are professional-grade vinyl installed by our partners at Signorama. Zero paint damage. Vehicles are maintained and driven by verified, insured drivers. We don't place wraps on unroadworthy or poorly maintained cars."

#### If lead asks about minimum commitment:
> "3 months minimum. Most brands start with 3 and renew. Longer commitments (6 or 12 months) get better per-car pricing. Volume discounts at 10+ cars regardless of term."

#### If lead says "too expensive":
> "Fair point. A Standard wrap at R4,675/car/month is R156/day per car. For that, your brand is seen by roughly 3,000 people per hour, driving through suburbs your actual customers live in — not just highways. Compare that CPM to static billboards or digital display and the value becomes clearer. Happy to walk through the math on a call."

#### If lead asks for custom pricing / special deal:
> **[HANDOVER TRIGGER]** "That's a great question — I need to loop in our Director who handles custom enterprise packages. Let me connect you with Fulu directly. He'll be in touch within 24 hours."
